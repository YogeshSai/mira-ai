    How funds are ranked: Funds are ranked within their own category over the period you pick (1Y, 3Y, 5Y, 10Y or since inception), using only funds that have at least that much history and a recent NAV. Each fund gets a score out of 100 from six factors: return a year (30%), Sharpe ratio (20%), Sortino ratio (15%), maximum drawdown (15%), standard deviation (10%) and trend (10%). For the first five, the fund’s value is turned into a percentile against its category peers, where a higher return or ratio and a smaller drawdown or lower volatility score better. The trend factor is the 0–100 trend score from the NAV against its 14, 100 and 200-day EMA. Rank 1 has the highest score, and ties go to the higher return. Sharpe and Sortino use the risk-free rate set on the fund page (6.5% by default). Benchmark-based measures such as beta, alpha and capture ratios are not used, because they depend on the benchmark chosen. “Return only” ranks on return a year alone. Rankings use past data, which does not predict future performance.
